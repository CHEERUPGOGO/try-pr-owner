Key Idea
The outer layer employs an evolutionary algorithm (EA) to evolve the weight vector α, while the inner
layer utilizes a nested greedy algorithm (NGA) combined with a surrogate heuristic function to rapidly construct
tool subsets Finally, an LLM performs the actual fitness evaluation.
